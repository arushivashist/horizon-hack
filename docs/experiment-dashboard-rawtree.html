Naive agent: history + summaries

  • Keeps one conversation for the whole month: every page, query and result stays in view.
  • Re-reads all of it on every call, so each step gets heavier as the month goes on.
  • At 30k tokens it squashes everything into a summary and starts growing again.
  • What survives depends on what the summary happened to keep; there's no record of why it believes something.
  • Anything it read, including a bad log line, stays in context until the next summary.

Dejavu agent: explicit, checked memory

  • Starts every page fresh: the system prompt, its memory, and the new alert. Nothing else from earlier pages.
  • Keeps full detail within a page (up to 15 queries), then throws that page's conversation away.
  • Carries forward only short lessons: at most 12 lessons and 3 false-alarm notes of 400 characters each.
  • Every write is checked: it must cite pages the agent closed; nothing is written mid-investigation; instructions, links and credentials are refused.
  • Every edit goes to an append-only log, so memory can be replayed as of any day or rolled back.
  • It edits and retires lessons as evidence changes; on day 24 it checked its connection-limit lesson and didn't apply it blindly.

Where it isn't efficient yet: the Dejavu agent made 258 model calls to the naive agent's 99, because it updates its memory after every page and every end-of-day note. Both read about the same total (1.63M vs 1.65M tokens), but the naive agent's growing history is cheap to re-read from cache, so it cost $2.40 against $6.07. The planned fix is to hand memory upkeep and false-alarm triage to a small Liquid model.

What we learned

  • Accuracy tied. Both agents got all 39 pages right, including the day-24 look-alike. At 30 days, a strong model with good summaries holds up.
  • The naive agent was faster and cheaper here: fewer queries, and its growing history is cheap to re-read thanks to caching.
  • The Dejavu agent's context has a hard ceiling, about a third of the naive agent's peak, and it doesn't depend on when a summary happens to run.
  • Its memory is auditable: every lesson cites the pages behind it and can be replayed as of any day. On day 22 it reused its own day-4 lesson, and on day 24 it checked that lesson against the evidence instead of applying it blindly.
