What we learned

  • Accuracy tied. Both agents got all 39 pages right, including the day-24 look-alike. At 30 days, a strong model with good summaries holds up.
  • The naive agent was faster and cheaper here: fewer queries, and its growing history is cheap to re-read thanks to caching.
  • The memory agent's context has a hard ceiling, about a third of the naive agent's peak, and it doesn't depend on when a summary happens to run.
  • Its memory is auditable: every lesson cites the pages behind it and can be replayed as of any day. On day 22 it reused its own day-4 lesson, and on day 24 it checked that lesson against the evidence instead of applying it blindly.
